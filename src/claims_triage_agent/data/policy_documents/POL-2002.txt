COASTAL BASIC BRONZE HMO -- POLICY DOCUMENT (SYNTHETIC, FOR DEMO PURPOSES ONLY)

Section 1 - Overview
This document describes coverage rules for the Coastal Basic Bronze HMO
plan that are not captured in the structured benefits table, including
exclusions and prior-authorization requirements.

Section 3 - Imaging Services: Brain MRI
Brain MRI (CPT 70551) requires prior authorization before the procedure
is performed, except when the MRI is ordered directly from an emergency
department encounter, in which case prior authorization is waived
provided the claim documents the emergency department visit and, where
available, a retrospective authorization reference number. Claims for
CPT 70551 that lack both a prior authorization number and documented
emergency-department context will be flagged for manual review rather
than automatically approved.

Section 5 - Exclusions: Cosmetic Consultations
Office visits (CPT 99213) held solely for elective cosmetic
consultations, with no other medically necessary complaint documented,
are not covered under this plan.

Section 8 - Network Requirements
This is an HMO plan. Services rendered by out-of-network providers are
not covered except in a documented emergency, regardless of whether the
procedure code itself is on the covered-procedures list.
