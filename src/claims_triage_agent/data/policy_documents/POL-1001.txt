MERIDIAN HEALTH GOLD PPO -- POLICY DOCUMENT (SYNTHETIC, FOR DEMO PURPOSES ONLY)

Section 1 - Overview
This document describes coverage rules for the Meridian Health Gold PPO
plan that are not captured in the structured benefits table, including
exclusions and prior-authorization requirements.

Section 4 - Exclusions: Skin Lesion Removal
Removal of benign skin lesions, including skin tags (CPT 11200), is
covered only when the lesion causes documented irritation, bleeding,
infection, or functional impairment, as noted by the treating physician.
Removal performed for purely cosmetic reasons, with no such symptoms
documented, is excluded from coverage under this plan regardless of
whether CPT 11200 appears on the standard covered-procedures list.

Section 6 - Prior Authorization: Joint Replacement
Total knee replacement (CPT 27447) and total hip replacement (CPT 27130)
require prior authorization from Meridian Health Gold PPO's utilization
management team at least 14 calendar days before the scheduled procedure
date. Claims for these procedures submitted without a documented prior
authorization reference number will be denied.

Section 7 - Repeat Procedures: Same-Joint Arthroscopy
Knee arthroscopy (CPT 29881) performed on the same joint within 90 days
of a prior approved arthroscopy on that same joint is not automatically
payable. These claims require a peer-to-peer medical necessity review
between the treating physician and a Meridian medical director before
payment can be issued, and should be flagged for manual review rather
than auto-approved or auto-denied.

Section 9 - Physical Therapy Visit Limits
Outpatient physical therapy (CPT 97110) is covered up to 20 visits per
calendar year without prior authorization. Additional visits beyond the
20-visit limit require a submitted treatment plan and prior
authorization.
